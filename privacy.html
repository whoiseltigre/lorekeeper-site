Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Lorekeeper makes fandom quiz videos for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This page explains what data we handle. The short version: this website collects nothing, and our only app, the Lorekeeper Uploader, is used solely to manage our own YouTube channel.

This website

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

The Lorekeeper Uploader and YouTube API Services

The Lorekeeper Uploader is an internal tool that uses YouTube API Services to publish videos to the Lorekeeper YouTube channel and read that channel’s own analytics. It is not offered to the public and is used only by the channel owner.

  • What it accesses: the Lorekeeper channel’s basic details, the videos we upload, and that channel’s aggregate analytics (such as views and how much of each video people watched).
  • How it is used: to upload and schedule our videos, and to decide which segments of our own videos to turn into Shorts.
  • What it does not do: it does not access any viewer’s personal data, other people’s channels, or anything outside the Lorekeeper channel. We never sell or share this data.
  • Storage: the access token is stored as an encrypted secret in our private build system. Analytics numbers are stored in our private database and kept only as long as they are useful for planning videos.

By using YouTube, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube’s handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the Lorekeeper Uploader can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Social media

When you watch, like or comment on our videos, the platform you are using (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram) handles that data under its own privacy policy. We only see the aggregate statistics those platforms give every creator.
