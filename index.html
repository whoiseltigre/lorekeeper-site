At his farewell party in The Fellowship of the Ring, how old does Bilbo turn?

Eleventy-one. The other three are traps from the same book: 33 is Frodo, who shares the birthday; 144 is the number of guests at the special dinner, one gross, and their combined age; 131 is how old Bilbo is when he finally sails from the Grey Havens.

As told in The Fellowship of the Ring, Book I, chapter 1, “A Long-expected Party”.
